# Hackathon demonstration guide

1. Open the website. Show the neural core and the five supported formats.
2. Launch the dashboard. Choose Explore demo cases. These are clearly labeled synthetic evidence, not fictional results masquerading as processed files.
3. Open Damaged laptop delivery. Wait for all three files to become Ready.
4. Open Evidence Library and preview the actual invoice PDF and event CSV.
5. Ask “What may have caused the laptop damage?” With Gemini configured, inspect observations, hypotheses, conflicting information, missing information and next checks. Without Gemini, explicitly demonstrate source retrieval only.
6. Click a citation. Show the original page/CSV row and evidence ID.
7. Open Timeline. Contrast the courier's intact entry and the later complaint without concluding fraud or causation.
8. Open Knowledge Graph. Select a source or supported mention edge.
9. Generate and download the PDF report.
10. Open Machine A. Show the calculated vibration/temperature charts and maintenance PDF. Ask about the stop; keep all engineering explanations tentative.
11. Create an empty workspace and upload your own files. This proves results are driven by evidence rather than scripted answers.

For actual video citations, upload a staged clip from the asset checklist, install FFmpeg and configure Gemini. Do not show a fabricated video citation. Education's study workflow is in Insights, and its quizzes/flashcards require Gemini. All final operational/claims decisions remain with people.
