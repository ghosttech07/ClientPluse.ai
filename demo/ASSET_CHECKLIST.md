# Synthetic media asset checklist

The included PDFs, CSV logs, and text statements are genuine input files processed by the same pipeline as user uploads. No AI responses are prewritten. Media is optional and must be uploaded separately; the prepared cases never claim to contain video or audio that is absent.

For the delivery case:
- A staged package photograph (PNG/JPG): crushed outer corner, synthetic label ORD-1042. Record capture time in an accompanying CSV.
- A short staged warehouse video (MP4, under 120 seconds and 25 MB): one visible handling event. Include a SYNTHETIC watermark. Avoid implying the video establishes the laptop's internal condition.
- A narrated customer statement (MP3/WAV): read the supplied statement, explicitly saying it is fictional.

For manufacturing:
- A staged equipment image, synthetic CV-204 label.
- A short simulation video with SYNTHETIC watermark. A visible stop is an observation; an internal mechanical cause is not.
- A narrated technician statement matching the supplied text.

Do not use real customer or workplace footage without permission. Gemini is needed for image/audio/video understanding; FFmpeg is required to check short-video duration. Images are not generated in this build. Delete a synthetic workspace in Settings, then use Explore demo cases to recreate it.
